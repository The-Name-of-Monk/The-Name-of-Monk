ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

What is in a
Name?

The word "Monk" has traveled a long distance through history. It began in the stone cloisters of the Middle Ages, signifying a life of absolute discipline, silence, and withdrawal from the world. Yet, centuries later, we find the same name stamped on the leather soles of gentleman’s footwear and spinning on the turntables of smoky jazz clubs in New York.

This exhibition, "The Name of Monk", uses the polysemy of “Monk” to trace a trajectory from asceticism, to utility, to liberalism—three modes through which human beings have regulated, shaped, and ultimately released the body, knowledge, sound, and identity.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

This website is a Multimedia Museum Meta Mirror (MMMM)—a digital companion designed to guide you through a curated collection of physical artifacts. Our exhibition, "The Name of Monk," traces the fascinating semantic and cultural trajectory of a single word—Monk.

How to Visit: Unlike a traditional museum where you might stroll randomly, this digital experience allows you to choose your own path. We have organized the 16 exhibition items into two distinct Narratives, changing the order and context in which you encounter the items.

By clicking the "Start the Exhibition" button, you can choose your preferred narrative. You can also view all the exhibition items in the Collections by clicking on the images to the right, or "Go to the Map" to see exhibition items in the virtual rooms.
