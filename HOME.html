ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

What is in a
Name?

The word "Monk" has traveled a long distance through history. It began in the stone cloisters of the Middle Ages, signifying a life of absolute discipline, silence, and withdrawal from the world. Yet, centuries later, we find the same name stamped on the leather soles of gentleman’s footwear and spinning on the turntables of smoky jazz clubs in New York.

This exhibition, "The Name of the Monk", uses the polysemy of “Monk” to trace a trajectory from asceticism, to utility, to liberalism—three modes through which human beings have regulated, shaped, and ultimately released the body, knowledge, sound, and identity.

We invite visitors to walk a path that leads from the Sacred to the Secular, and finally to the Subversive. Through 15 carefully selected artifacts—from the rigid Rule of Saint Benedict to the improvised rhythms of Thelonious Monk—we trace how humanity has moved from the strict adherence to collective rules toward the celebration of individual freedom.

Welcome to the evolution of the Monk.
