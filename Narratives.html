Narrative I: The Evolution

The Path of History. Follow the linear timeline of the "Monk" concept. This narrative guides you chronologically from the medieval Rule of Saint Benedict (c. 530 AD), through the craftsmanship of 18th-century buckle makers, to the mid-20th century cultural explosion of Bebop Jazz. Perfect for historians and those who love to see cause and effect.

Narrative II: Mental Discourse

The Path of Ideas. Step away from dates and dive into the philosophical shift. This narrative groups artifacts by their inner meaning: The Sacred (Rules & Silence), The Secular (Work & Commerce), and The Subversive (Art & Improvisation). This path explores how humanity moved from collective discipline to individual freedom.
